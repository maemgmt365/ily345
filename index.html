why three numbers

most of us stop saying it once we leave home.

kids hear it every day. then we grow up, move out, get busy, and it gets quiet. only one in five adults tells their mom they love her daily. nearly half of men would rather not say it to their dad or their brother at all. between friends it is harder still. the words are there. the moment never feels right.

345 is the easier way to say them. a sign-off on a text. a hat on a friend. a hoodie you hand to your brother, your mom, your kid, without having to explain anything.

the easy way to say the hard thing.
