where the money goes

the ledger.

345 is not a business we are trying to grow. it is a way to say three things, and a way to give to something that matters to us. every piece sends a fixed amount to a mental health nonprofit. this page shows the number, the math behind it, and the receipts.

nothing kept.
why

this part is personal

the person 345 started with lived with depression, and with the things depression drags in behind it. she also spent years working in halfway houses and in homes for kids who needed someone steady, and as a special education para. she was good at showing up for people who were struggling.

so this is where the money goes. not to us. to the kind of help she gave, and the kind she needed.

who

the partner

we are looking at a second partner that fits the rest of her story, around disability and the homes she worked in. when that is set, it will be listed here with its own share.

how much

the number on every piece

here is what each piece costs to make and send, what the card processor takes, sales tax on the blank, a flat 5% that keeps the site running, and what is left. what is left goes to the partner. we round it down to the dollar on the product pages so we never promise more than we send.

printful is our print partner; their cost includes the blank, the print and the embroidery. the 5% covers the domain, email and tools. anything the 5% does not spend is added to the transfer. shipping is paid by the buyer and goes straight to printful. orders placed with a friends-and-family code are at cost and do not count.

when

once a year, in december

sales sit in stripe until december. then the whole balance owed goes out in one transfer, the receipt gets posted here, and the counter resets. small brand, one clean number a year.

the honest part

a few straight answers

is my purchase tax-deductible? no. you bought a hoodie at its normal price. the donation is made by 345's founder, personally, so the receipt and the deduction are his. if you want your own receipt, give to the partner directly; the link is on their card above.

does the founder make money on this? no, and that is the point. 345 was never built to be an income. the goal is to get three words said more often, in more houses, between people who mean them. the prices cover what the pieces cost to make and send, the partner gets what is left, and the founder gets to see the message travel.

why not 100% of proceeds? because "proceeds" can mean anything. a dollar figure per piece is a promise you can check.

what if costs change? the table updates and the date on it changes. the number on the product page is always the current one.

can i see the receipts? yes, every transfer is logged above with its date, amount and the partner's receipt.
